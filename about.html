About our History

Founded in 2012, 101 Bagels and Subs started as a small, family-owned café just a short walk from the scenic coastline of Oceanside, California. Our founders, longtime locals with a passion for good food and community, wanted to create a place where everyone—from early-morning surfers to weekend families—could enjoy a fresh, satisfying meal made with care. Inspired by the classic flavors of East Coast bagel shops and the laid-back spirit of Southern California, we opened our doors with one simple goal: to serve the best bagels and sandwiches in town. Over the years, our commitment to quality ingredients, friendly service, and consistency has helped us grow from a humble neighborhood spot into a local favorite. Today, 101 Bagels and Subs is proud to be known as one of the best places in Oceanside to grab a hearty breakfast, a freshly made sub, or a quick coffee before heading out to enjoy the California sunshine.

About Our Goals

Our goals for 101 Bagel and Subs in Oceanside, CA are to establish a strong local presence by becoming the go-to neighborhood spot for fresh, high-quality bagels and sandwiches while delivering exceptional quality and consistency every day. The shop aims to create a welcoming coastal vibe that reflects Oceanside’s relaxed, beach-town charm and offers fast, friendly service even during busy rush hours. By expanding the menu to include diverse options such as vegetarian, vegan, and specialty items, the shop hopes to appeal to a wide range of customers. Building strong community connections through local partnerships and events, strengthening online visibility, and encouraging repeat business through loyalty programs are also key priorities. Ultimately, the goal is to achieve steady, sustainable growth and become Oceanside’s favorite destination for fresh bagels, great subs, and a warm, inviting atmosphere.
